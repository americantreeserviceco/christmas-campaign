Cut trees: keep them drinking

  • Make a fresh cut across the base before the tree goes in the stand, and keep the stand filled with water.
  • Set the tree away from radiators, heat vents, fireplaces, TV sets and direct sun. A well-watered tree dries out more slowly.
  • Choose small lights. They run cooler than large bulbs.

A well-watered tree is far less likely to catch fire than one with dry needles, according to the Colorado State Forest Service.

Living trees: after the holidays

A tree in a pot or with a root ball needs water through the winter, the same as any evergreen. The watering rules on the Thanksgiving page apply: water when the air is above 40°F and the soil is not frozen, and avoid ground covered in snow.

For care of a living tree that has been indoors, see the CSU Extension article below.
