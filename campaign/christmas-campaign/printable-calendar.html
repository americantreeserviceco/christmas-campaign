12 Days of Tree-mendous Cheer

December 13–24, 2026 · A little tree care. A lot of Christmas cheer.

DEC 13 DAY 1

Welcome to the countdown

Santa Dr. Troy is bringing 12 days of tree-mendous cheer! Join American Tree Colorado for a little winter tree care, a few festive questions, and plenty of Colorado Christmas spirit. What is your favorite tree in your yard?

DEC 14 DAY 2

A winter drink, when conditions allow

Day 2: A little winter care goes a long way. During dry stretches, trees may need supplemental water. Choose snow-free conditions with unfrozen ground, and wait until both air and soil temperatures exceed 40°F.

DEC 15 DAY 3

Give roots a cozy blanket

Day 3: Mulch is a useful winter helper. A 2–4 inch layer of organic mulch can moderate soil conditions; keep it clear of the trunk. Think blanket, not a pile against the bark.

DEC 16 DAY 4

Remember the younger trees

Day 4: The little trees deserve a place on the nice list. Recently planted trees have smaller developing root systems and need extra attention during winter dry spells.

DEC 17 DAY 5

Check the bark

Day 5: Winter sunshine and temperature swings can stress young, thin-barked trees. Ask about appropriate trunk protection for your tree rather than guessing at the right material or timing.

DEC 18 DAY 6

Evergreens need care, too

Day 6: Still green does not mean care-free. Evergreens can also benefit from winter moisture during extended dry conditions. Keep their needs in mind when checking the landscape.

DEC 19 DAY 7

Watch the dry spots

Day 7: Sun, wind, and reflected heat can leave some landscape spots drier than others. Check exposed areas instead of assuming every tree has the same winter moisture needs.

DEC 20 DAY 8

Leave the big jobs to a pro

Day 8: Santa Dr. Troy’s wish list includes sensible tree-care plans. If a tree job is beyond your skills or equipment, get professional help. A holiday countdown is no reason to rush a big job.

DEC 21 DAY 9

Plan before you prune

Day 9: Put thoughtful pruning on the calendar. Dormant-season pruning suits many trees, but the right timing and cuts depend on the tree and its condition. Make a plan before reaching for the tools.

DEC 22 DAY 10

Show us your Colorado Christmas tree

Day 10: Let’s make the comments a little more tree-mendous. Share a photo of a favorite tree or holiday tree display. Big, small, indoors, or outdoors—tell us why it makes you smile.

DEC 23 DAY 11

Put next year’s tree care on the list

Day 11: A new year is almost here. Have a tree question you have been putting off? Tell American Tree Colorado what you are noticing, and ask about a tree-care assessment.

DEC 24 DAY 12

A tree-mendous Christmas Eve

Day 12: That is a wrap on our Christmas countdown! Thank you for sharing your questions, your trees, and your Colorado holiday spirit. From American Tree Colorado and Santa Dr. Troy, wishing you a joyful Christmas Eve.
